===============================================================================
ORCHESTRA-CORE - DEMO COPY
===============================================================================

This is the whole website with NO BACKEND. Nothing here is real: no database,
no M-Pesa, no emails. Every button works, but it is all pretend.

Use it to look around, to show people, and to decide what you want changed.


HOW TO OPEN IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser and works offline.

To put it on a laptop or phone for someone else, copy this whole folder and
they do the same. No installation, no internet needed.


THE BAR AT THE BOTTOM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
There is a black-edged bar along the bottom of every page. That is the demo
switcher - it does not exist on the real website.

It lets you become five different people:

  Visitor       Signed out. Can browse, cannot read a lesson.
  Free account  Reads the first lesson of each series, paywall on the rest.
  Full access   Paid. Every lesson open, and has sessions booked.
  Consultant    An approved teacher, with availability, sessions and earnings.
  Admin         You. Approvals, payouts, refunds and the inbox.

Click a name to switch. It takes you to the page that person cares about.
Press the X to hide the bar for a clean screenshot; the round "Demo" button in
the corner brings it back.


WHAT TO TRY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As VISITOR        the homepage, /lessons, /pricing, /consultants
                  try to open a lesson - you get the sign-up gate

as FREE ACCOUNT   open module 1 of any series - it reads
                  open module 2 - you hit the paywall
                  go to /pricing and buy it (the M-Pesa prompt is faked and
                  confirms itself after about 5 seconds)

as FULL ACCESS    the dashboard with all 80 lessons
                  book a consultant at /consultants - pick a time, pay
                  /sessions - cancel one, or report a no-show

as CONSULTANT     /teach/dashboard - your pay, earnings, availability
                  add an availability window and save it
                  mark a past session delivered

as ADMIN          /admin - approve a teacher and set their three pay figures
                  the payouts tab, the refunds tab, the inbox


WHAT IS REAL AND WHAT IS NOT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REAL   - every page, button, layout and word
       - all 80 lessons, in full - you can read them
       - the rules: who can read what, what gets refunded, how pay is worked out

FAKE   - the teachers, learners, bookings, messages and money
       - the M-Pesa prompts (they confirm themselves after a few seconds)
       - anything you change resets when you reload the page


TELLING ME WHAT TO CHANGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Just say where it is and what is wrong. For example:

  "On the consultants page, 'Book a consultant' should say 'Find a teacher'."
  "The About page is too empty, add back the bit about being a student."
  "Make the price bigger on the homepage."
  "The teacher dashboard should show the learner's phone number."

Page addresses are in the browser bar after the # sign, which makes them easy
to quote.

Changes get made to the real website, then this demo folder is rebuilt from the
same code - so the two can never disagree.


REBUILDING THIS FOLDER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
From the main project folder:

    npm run build:demo

That regenerates everything here from the current code.
